This chapter covers Simultaneous Localization and Mapping (SLAM), a critical capability for robots to navigate unknown environments. We will cover:
- SLAM algorithms and principles
- LiDAR-based SLAM (e.g., Cartographer)
- Visual SLAM
- Integrating SLAM into ROS 2 applications
